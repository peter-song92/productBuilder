Why QuickTip exists

QuickTip was built for one simple reason: tipping math should never slow down a great meal. We created a fast, clean calculator that respects local tax rates and makes totals easy to understand. It is designed for people who want clarity and speed without wading through cluttered apps or confusing calculators.

How the calculator works

The calculator separates pre‑tax and after‑tax totals. If you enter a pre‑tax amount, we apply tax and tips directly. If you enter an after‑tax total, we reverse the tax to estimate the pre‑tax amount first. This keeps the tip based on the pre‑tax value, which is the most common tipping method in the U.S.

What we value

  • Clarity over complexity. The numbers should explain themselves.
  • Consistency across devices. The UI is optimized for phones first.
  • Respect for users. No tricks, no confusing defaults.

Coverage and updates

QuickTip includes common tax presets and a custom option so you can match your local rate. We regularly review content and adjust guides to keep them accurate and useful. If you see something that looks off, please let us know.
